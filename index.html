FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

All You Need to Know

How do my donations directly help the planet?

Your contributions fund frontline tree plantings with the Canopy Project, support community beach and river cleanups, provide climate literacy materials to underfunded schools, and drive legal policy advocacy across the globe.

Is EARTHDAY.ORG a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit?

Yes. EARTHDAY.ORG (Earth Day Network) is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit charity in the United States. Contributions are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by law, and we maintain a 4-star Charity Navigator rating.

What is the theme for Earth Day 2027?

The official global theme for Earth Day 2027 is Food for Our Planet™, focusing on transforming agriculture, supporting regenerative farming practices, eliminating food waste, and fostering global food security.

How can schools and organizations get involved?

Visit our Earth Hub to download free educational toolkits, register a Great Global Cleanup event, or join our global network of over 150,000 schools and community organizers.

HAVE ANY QUESTIONS?

Our team in Washington, D.C. and across 6 regional hubs is ready to assist your organization or campaign.
